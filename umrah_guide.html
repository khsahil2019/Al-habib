STEP 1 OF 4

Step 1: Entering Ihram & Making Niyyah at Miqat

VERIFIED SOURCE
Preparation Rules:
  1. Ghusl / Wudu: Perform Ghusl (full bath) or Wudu before entering Ihram.
  2. Ihram Garments:
    • Men: Two unstitched white sheets (Rida for upper body, Izar for lower body). Wear slippers that leave ankles and upper foot bare.
    • Women: Regular modest Islamic clothing with Hijab (face bare, no Niqab touching face while in Ihram).
  3. Sunnat-ul-Ihram: Offer 2 Rak'at Sunnah prayer before forming Niyyah (if not in a makruh prayer time).
  4. Apply Perfume (Sunnah before Niyyah): Men may apply perfume to body before Niyyah, but not after.
DUA 1 — UMRAH NIYYAH إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أُرِيدُ الْعُمْرَةَ فَيَسِّرْهَا لِي وَتَقَبَّلْهَا مِنِّي

Transliteration: Allahumma inni ureedul- 'umrata fayassirhaa lee wa taqabbalhaa minnee.

English: "O Allah, I intend to perform Umrah, so make it easy for me and accept it from me."

Hindi: "ऐ अल्लाह! मैं उमराह की नीयत करता हूँ, इसे मेरे लिए आसान फरमा और इसे मेरी तरफ से क़बूल फरमा।"

DUA 2 — THE TALBIYAH
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لاَ شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لاَ شَرِيكَ لَكَ

Transliteration: Labbayk Allahumma Labbayk, Labbayka laa shareeka laka Labbayk, Innal-hamda wan-ni'mata laka wal-mulk, Laa shareeka lak.

English: "Here I am at Your service O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. Truly all praise, grace, and sovereignty belong to You. You have no partner."

Hindi: "मैं हाज़िर हूँ ऐ अल्लाह, मैं हाज़िर हूँ! तेरा कोई शरीक नहीं, मैं हाज़िर हूँ! बेशक तमाम तारीफें, नीमतें और सल्तनत तेरी ही हैं, तेरा कोई शरीक नहीं।"

STEP 2 OF 4

Step 2: Entering Al-Masjid al-Haram & Performing Tawaf

VERIFIED SOURCE
DUA 3 — ENTERING AL-MASJID AL-HARAM
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَالصَّلاَةُ وَالسَّلاَمُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ

Transliteration: Bismillahi was-salaatu was-salaamu 'alaa rasoolillaah, Allahummaf-tah lee abwaaba rahmatik.

English: "In the name of Allah, and peace and blessings be upon the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy."

DUA 4 — FIRST SIGHT OF KAABA SHARIF
اللَّهُمَّ زِدْ هَذَا الْبَيْتَ تَشْرِيفًا وَتَعْظِيمًا وَتَكْرِيمًا وَمَهَابَةً

Transliteration: Allahumma zid haadhal-bayta tashreefaw-wa ta'theemaw-wa takreemaw-wa mahaabah.

English: "O Allah, increase this House in honor, esteem, respect, and awe." (Any sincere Dua made upon first sight of Kaaba is accepted).

Rules of Tawaf (7 Circuits):
  • Wudu Required: Complete Wudu is mandatory for Tawaf.
  • Idtiba' (Men only): Uncover right shoulder by passing upper Ihram sheet under right armpit.
  • Raml (Men only): Brisk walking with short steps in the first 3 circuits of Tawaf.
  • Start Line (Green Light): Align with Hajar al-Aswad (Black Stone) marked by the green light on wall. Raise hands towards stone & say:
    بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ (Bismillahi Allāhu Akbar).
  • Any Zikr / Quran: During Tawaf, you may recite any Dua, Quran, or Istighfar. There are no fixed compulsory Duas for each circuit.
  • Cover Right Shoulder After 7 Circuits: Once 7 circuits are complete, cover right shoulder immediately.
DUA 5 — BETWEEN RUKN AL-YAMANI & HAJAR AL-ASWAD (Recited in Every Circuit)
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Transliteration: Rabbanaa aatinaa fid-dunyaa hasanataw-wa fil-aakhirati hasanataw-wa qinaa 'adhaaban-naar.

English: "Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and save us from the punishment of the Fire." (Sahih Bukhari 4522)

Hindi: "ऐ हमारे रब! हमें दुनिया में भी भलाई दे और आख़िरत में भी भलाई दे और हमें आग के अज़ाब से बचा।"

2 Rak'at Salah at Maqam Ibrahim

Offer 2 Rak'at Wajib-ut-Tawaf behind Maqam Ibrahim (or anywhere in Haram if crowded). Recite Surah Al-Kafirun in 1st Rak'at and Surah Al-Ikhlas in 2nd Rak'at.

DUA 6 — DRINKING ZAMZAM WATER
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا وَرِزْقًا وَاسِعًا وَشِفَاءً مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ

Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman naafi'aw-wa rizqaw-waasi'aw-wa shifa'am-min kulli daa'. ("O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, abundant sustenance, and cure from every illness.")

STEP 3 OF 4

Step 3: Performing Sa'i Between Mount Safa & Marwa (7 Rounds)

VERIFIED SOURCE

Proceed to Mount Safa. One round is from Safa to Marwa (Round 1), and Marwa to Safa (Round 2). Total 7 rounds end at Mount Marwa.

DUA 7 — APPROACHING MOUNT SAFA
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ... أَبْدَأُ بِمَا بَدَأَ اللَّهُ بِهِ

Transliteration: Innas-safaa wal-marwata min sha'aa'irillaah... Abda'u bimaa bada'allaahu bih.

English: "Indeed, Safa and Marwa are among the symbols of Allah... I begin with what Allah began with." (Recited at Safa before 1st lap).

DUA 8 — STANDING ON MOUNT SAFA & MARWA (Facing Kaaba)
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لاَ شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

Transliteration: Allāhu Akbar, Allāhu Akbar, Allāhu Akbar. Laa ilaaha illallaahu wahdahu laa shareeka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamdu wa huwa 'alaa kulli shay'in qadeer.

English: "Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest. There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. To Him belongs sovereignty and praise, and He is over all things competent." (Recite 3 times with personal Duas in between).

DUA 9 — WHILE WALKING BETWEEN GREEN LIGHTS (Milain Akhdarain)
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ وَأَنْتَ الأَعَزُّ الأَكْرَمُ

Transliteration: Rabbigh-fir war-ham wa antal-a'azzul-akram.

English: "My Lord, forgive and have mercy, for You are the Most Mighty, the Most Generous." (Men jog briskly between green lights; women walk normally).

STEP 4 OF 4

Step 4: Halq or Qasr (Exiting the State of Ihram)

VERIFIED SOURCE
Hair Trimming Rules:
  • For Men:
    • Halq (Shaving): Shaving the entire head (Highly recommended Sunnah).
    • Qasr (Trimming): Trimming hair equally all around the head (at least 1 cm / fingertip length from every part).
  • For Women:
    • Gather hair and cut approximately a fingertip's length (about 1 inch / 2.5 cm) from the ends of hair. Women do NOT shave head.

Umrah Completed!

Alhamdulillah! Once your hair is shaved/trimmed, all Ihram restrictions are lifted. You may change into normal clothing and wear perfume.

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي بِنِعْمَتِهِ تَتِمُّ الصَّالِحَاتُ

Alhamdulillaahilladhee bi-ni'matihi tatimmus-saalihaat. ("Praise be to Allah by Whose grace good deeds are completed.")
