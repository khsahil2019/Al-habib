Standard 15-Day Umrah Itinerary

Lucknow & Delhi Flights
Days 1 to 7: Makkah Mukarramah
  • Day 1 — Lucknow → Jeddah ✈️ Lucknow departure, enter Ihram before parallel flight Miqat, Jeddah arrival, AC transfer to Makkah hotel check-in.
  • Day 2 — Sacred Umrah Execution 🕋 Rest, group Umrah guidance by Hafiz Yusuf Malik team, perform Tawaf, Sa'i, and Halq/Qasr. Umrah completed.
  • Day 3 & 4 — Makkah Ziyarat 🚌 Haram visits, personal ibadah, guided Ziyarat to Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah & Jabal al-Noor (Cave Hira view).
  • Days 5 to 7 — Ibadah & Nafl Tawaf Free days for Nafl Tawaf, Quran recitation, shopping, and personal prayers at Al-Masjid al-Haram.
Days 8 to 15: Madinah Munawwarah
  • Day 8 — Makkah → Madinah 🚌 Hotel checkout, travel by luxury AC bus to Madinah Munawwarah, check-in near Masjid an-Nabawi.
  • Day 9 & 10 — Madinah Ziyarat 🕌 Prayers at Al-Masjid an-Nabawi, guided Ziyarat to Masjid Quba (first mosque), Mount Uhud, and Masjid Qiblatain.
  • Days 11 to 14 — Rawdah & Ibadah Riaz ul-Jannah (Rawdah Mubarak) visit according to Nusuk permit booking, personal ibadah, and shopping.
  • Day 15 — Madinah → India ✈️ Final prayers, checkout, airport transfer, direct return flight, and safe arrival in India.
