Standard 15-Day Umrah Tour Itinerary

Request Itinerary PDF
Phase 1: Makkah Mukarramah (Days 1 – 7)
  • Day 1: Departure & Umrah Rituals
    Fly from Lucknow/Delhi to Saudi Arabia with Ihram assumed at Miqat line. Arrive at hotel, rest, and perform 1st Umrah Tawaf & Sa'i guided by group leader.
  • Day 2 – 4: Ibadaat in Haram Sharif
    Daily 5-time Congregational Prayers in Al-Masjid al-Haram, Nafl Tawaf, and spiritual lectures by Hafiz Yusuf Malik.
  • Day 5: Guided Makkah Ziyarat
    Visit Jabal al-Nour (Cave of Hira), Cave of Thawr, Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah, and Jannat al-Mu'alla in luxury AC buses.
  • Day 6 – 7: Optional 2nd Umrah via Tan'im
    Option for pilgrims to visit Masjid Aisha (Tan'im) to assume Ihram for an additional Umrah for deceased relatives.
Phase 2: Madinah Al-Munawwarah (Days 8 – 15)
  • Day 8: Transfer to Madinah Munawwarah
    Travel from Makkah to Madinah via luxury AC coach. Check into hotel near Al-Masjid an-Nabawi and offer Salam at Rawdah Sharif.
  • Day 9 – 11: Prayers & Rawdah Permits
    Prayers in Masjid an-Nabawi. Group assistance for securing official Nusuk app Rawdah Sharif permits for men and women.
  • Day 12: Guided Madinah Ziyarat
    Visit Mount Uhud, Masjid Quba (1st mosque in Islam), Masjid al-Qiblatayn, and the Seven Mosques battlefield site.
  • Day 13 – 15: Farewell Salam & Departure
    Perform Farewell Salam at Roza-e-Rasool ﷺ, receive 5L Zamzam bottle per pilgrim, and transfer to airport for direct flight home to Lucknow/Delhi.
